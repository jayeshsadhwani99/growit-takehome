# Motion — why

The screens are the same product with or without motion. The motion is only
there so a change of page, dialog, or theme does not pop.

## Rules

- Page changes fade and shift a few pixels. The sidebar and the phone bars
  stay put.
- A dialog fades its scrim and rises a few pixels as it opens.
- A theme change eases the surface colors and fades the shell. The stored
  choice does not wait on the animation.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` drops the movement. The colors can still ease.
